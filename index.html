✳ THE FIELD GUIDE

How to play

  1. Choose your battle. Training has no pressure; Boss mode brings tougher monsters and longer words.
  2. Type the glowing word. Each correct word swings your sword. Faster hits and longer combos deal more damage.
  3. Guard your health. In battle modes, every fourth mistake costs health. Time-out phases hit hard too.
  4. Choose your own story. Between fights, type moss, ember, or crown to change the next monster and your supplies.
  5. Learn from the fight. Your results train a Random Forest model that recommends a skill level and next difficulty.

Skill categories are trained on small, documented synthetic sample data for this project demonstration. They are learning signals—not a validated assessment.
